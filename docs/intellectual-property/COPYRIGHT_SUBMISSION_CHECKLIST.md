# Copyright submission checklist

- [ ] Obtain and compare the original FinSight PDF and complete report; it was not available in this work session.
- [ ] Verify legal names, IDs, and each contributor's specific work with all contributors.
- [ ] Resolve RV University's ownership position using current policies and signed agreements; do not assume assignment.
- [ ] Decide the applicant and authorship descriptions with qualified Indian IP counsel.
- [ ] Separate the original project source and history from later enhancements.
- [ ] Preserve repository URLs, commits, design source files, build records, and dated screenshots.
- [ ] Keep a list of AI-assisted material and review its applicable terms before describing authorship.
- [ ] Review direct and transitive third-party licences, fonts, icons, and notices.
- [ ] Remove credentials and private/user data from source and evidence bundles.
- [ ] Identify only original expressive work; do not claim generic finance concepts, common charts, or standard dashboard patterns.
- [ ] Confirm the correct filing category, forms, deposit requirements, and current process with official Indian Copyright Office guidance or legal counsel.
- [ ] Make no statement that registration is guaranteed.
